We explored the form of the actor, visited a small planet, and became intimately familiar with Macbeth. Honestly though, this was a really fun class! I enjoyed it :)

From the course catalog: Explores the actor's dramatic instincts with body, voice, imagination, language, and action. Studio exercises foster creative curiosity, improvisation, collaboration, and a sense of kinesthetic and spatial awareness. Develops communication skills and offers varied approaches to relating with self, ensemble, character, dramatic material, and audience.
