The NEET Autonomous Machines capstone course provides seniors the opportunity to apply their skills, knowledge, and conceptual understanding of robotics and autonomous systems to develop a fully functional working project that involves aspects of design, control, simulation, perception, algorithm implementation, and hardware and software integration.
